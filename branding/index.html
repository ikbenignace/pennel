Musli · six directions

Dictation anywhere you type and meeting notes that stay yours. Six logo directions in the existing warm-paper language: Figtree for the interface, Instrument Serif for headings, one burnt-orange accent. Each card shows the mark, the lockup, the app icon from the 1024 master down to 32 px, the 18 pt menubar template on light and dark bars, the Dock, the listening pill and a landing hero.

Recommendation: 06 Full Stop as the identity (wordmark, app icon, site), with 01 Pulse as the menubar icon and listening state. “Zeg het. Punt uit.” works the same way in Dutch and English, the serif matches the headings already in the app, and the full stop says “finished, polished text”. The serif “m” is the weakest shape at 18 pt, and the bars are the strongest, so the menubar uses the bars. See palette.md and each folder’s notes.md.
